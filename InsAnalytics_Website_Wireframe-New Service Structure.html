Notes — Capabilities

  • The old structure led with delivery mode (Consulting / R&D / Product / Training) — the new one leads with outcome (predictive maintenance, reliability analytics, marketing budget impact...) and pushes delivery mode down to a small tag. This is the change you asked for.
  • List-length problem is solved structurally: the 7 pillars are fixed and rarely change; individual solutions inside each pillar can grow to 20+ over time without the nav ever getting longer.
  • Training stays a standalone pillar rather than folding into Digital Transformation, per your call — different buyer, different budget line, deserves its own front door.
  • Every solution card still carries the Requirement → Approach → Business benefit structure from your corporate deck — only the framing and grouping changed, not the underlying content.
